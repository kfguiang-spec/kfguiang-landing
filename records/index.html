Records

If you like Nathan Micay & Barker…

A branching record store: six aisles of real releases that fork off from two artists who share a love of euphoric, melodic electronic music but come at it from opposite sides. Micay works with breaks, trance and film-score strings; Barker makes techno with the kick drum taken out. Tap a sleeve to flip it for notes and links. The dashed sleeves at the end of a bin are where to go deeper.

leans Micay leans Barker where they meet

    Every release was checked against label, store or reference listings (LuckyMe, Smalltown Supersound, Apple Music, Bleep, Discogs, Wikipedia, Pitchfork, RA). Years and labels refer to the original release; where a sleeve image comes from a later reissue, the card says so.

    Artwork is official cover art from the Apple iTunes Search API. Records without artwork use a plain typographic sleeve marked “placeholder”.
