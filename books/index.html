    How it evolved

      2019–2020: markets. 13 of the 22 books dated 2019 were about investing or Wall Street history, and 9 of the 19 in 2020.

      2021–2022: a lull, then a wider mix. Only 4 books are dated 2021. In 2022 (11 books) finance was still the biggest group (5), but sports and food turned up for the first time.

      2023: the turn to biology. Of 18 books, none were about finance. Biotech, genetics and medicine led with 7, most of them read in June and July, followed by sports (5) and health (3).

      2024: the biggest year. 33 books, the last one finished in mid-August. Health, brain and longevity (7) and sports (7) led, then biotech (6), a run of four wine books in July and August, and AI (4). Only 3 of the 33 were about finance.

      Next: the to-read list points the same way. It is mostly sports and football (7), AI (6), biotech (4) and the raw materials behind technology (3).
